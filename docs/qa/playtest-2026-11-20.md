# Playtest triage: QA Central #10775 (2026-11-19/20)

**Source:** Danzo's Discord request on QA Central (posts 19-20 Nov), plus his summary file, given to the cloud session
on 2026-11-22. Testers: XTRA, Mambo, and one who could not play because of the 16+ rating. Scores: "pretty fun",
"very fun and very fast-paced", **7/10 "for the lack of content"**. Nobody faulted the core gameplay.

**Build unknown.** All of H37 (lobby), H38 (dash-tag) and H39 (roofs) were committed on 18 Nov, 17:10-18:54 UTC. The
repo does not record publishes. Danzo to confirm when he last published, because item 1 reads differently either
way.

## Suggested order

1. **Danzo confirms the published build** (before or after 18 Nov 19:00 UTC).
2. **#1 + #2 together as one heavy handoff** ("the chase"): measure tag misses on two real devices, then tune reach,
   "it"'s speed and the dodge as one balance question. These are linked: a faster "it" hides a reach bug, and the dodge
   cancels both.
3. **#3 walls grace** is small. It can ride on the same handoff or follow it.
4. **#4, #5, #9** are routine clarity fixes. **#6 tutorial** is the biggest win for new players.
5. **#10** is Danzo's, and quick.
6. **#7, #8** are content: the art block and the rolls.
